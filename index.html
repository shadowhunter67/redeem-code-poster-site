Redeem Code Poster

A personal, non-commercial tool that collects publicly announced game redeem codes (Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero, Wuthering Waves) and posts short videos about new codes to its owner's own social channels, including YouTube.

The tool is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts or sign-in to the public.

Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher, Google or YouTube.
